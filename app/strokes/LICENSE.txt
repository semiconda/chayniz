Stroke-order data: credits and licences
=======================================

Three separately-licensed things live in this directory, all vendored so the app
has no runtime dependency on anyone else's server.

1. hanzi-writer.min.js — the renderer
   Hanzi Writer 3.7.3 by David Chanin and contributors, MIT.
   https://hanziwriter.org/  ·  https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer

2. data/*.json — the stroke outlines, one file per character
   hanzi-writer-data 2.0.1, under the Arphic Public License.
   https://github.com/chanind/hanzi-writer-data
   The outlines derive from Make Me a Hanzi (https://github.com/skishore/
   makemeahanzi), which derives them from Arphic Technology's fonts. Renamed
   here from <character>.json to <codepoint>.json; the contents are unchanged.

3. parts.json — the component breakdown and the etymology lines
   Extracted from dictionary.txt in Make Me a Hanzi, commit bddc96d41b, under the GNU
   LGPL v3. That file derives from the Unihan database and from CJKlib; the
   notice below covers both. https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi
   Modified here: cut down to the characters this app teaches, the components
   flattened out of the IDS strings, the definitions shortened to one clause.
